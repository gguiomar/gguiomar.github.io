Gonçalo Guiomar · Research Fellow, ETH AI Center & INI, University of Zurich
ResearchArtEssaysProjects

I work at the intersection of mechanistic interpretability and cognition: how biological and artificial systems learn internal representations of the world through action and inference. I combine computational neuroscience, reinforcement learning and latent-state modeling to study decision-making, reasoning and adaptive behavior. Currently in the labs of Valerio Mante and Benjamin Grewe. Previously PhD at the Champalimaud Foundation with Joseph Paton. Students: see open projects.
